I am in my final year at Shanghai Pinghe Bilingual School. I dance, I play floorball for the Pinghe Dragons, and for the past four years I have taught programming to beginners at a volunteer organization in Shanghai.

The projects collected here were not planned as a set. A shot model built on NHL data, a study of how language models react to small changes in a prompt, a quantization problem in a mathematics essay. Different subjects, and underneath them the same short list of questions about noise, measurement, and what a system's behaviour is actually made of. Somewhere in the middle of them I noticed that I care much more about the mechanism underneath than about the application on top.

I do not have a narrow specialism yet and would rather not claim one. What I do think is that the tools for doing this kind of work are changing quickly enough to change which questions are worth attempting at all, and I would like to be working where that matters.

If these share anything, it is that each one ends up asking what its measurement can and cannot resolve. The NHL model's ceiling turns out to be a missing variable rather than a weak algorithm. The Extended Essay asks how coarse a step you can take before the signal is gone. Pioneer separates a real effect from the sampler's own noise. The teaching pages ask the same thing about a person: where does an explanation stop resolving, and for whom.

02NHL shot modelling · 956,147 attempts

A shot model, and the ceiling it hit

Five models, scored on 200,820 held-out shots. They land within half a percentage point of each other. That is the finding rather than the disappointment: all five are trained on the same features, and goalkeeper position, defender angle and screening are not among them. The limit is the data, not the architecture.

03IB Mathematics Extended Essay · May 2026

A ratio that does not depend on the noise

Force a time series to move in fixed steps instead of arbitrary ones and you remove some of its jitter. How much turns out not to depend on how noisy the series was to begin with: the constrained oscillation and the unconstrained baseline both scale with the noise level in exactly the same way, so it divides out. Three lines of algebra, found by doing the derivation rather than by expecting it. One conjecture in the paper is still unproven and is labelled as such.

04Pioneer Research · LLM output stability

Separating a real effect from the sampler

Five kinds of meaning-preserving prompt change, applied to 200 items across four tasks, five generations each. The model already answers differently every time it is asked, so most of any observed difference is the sampler rather than the change being tested. The decomposition pulls the two apart. Rephrasing moves the centre furthest in all four tasks and holds in 11 of 12 profiles across three models. The twelfth is unresolved, and the paper says so.
