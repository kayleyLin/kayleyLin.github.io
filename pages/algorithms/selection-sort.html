Pseudocode
for i in range(n): min_idx = i for j in range(i+1, n): if arr[j] < arr[min_idx]: min_idx = j swap(arr[i], arr[min_idx]) // position i is now sorted
Complexity
Time , Best: O(n²) [always] Time , Average: O(n²) Time , Worst: O(n²) Space, O(1) [in-place] Stable: No
Complexity
TimeO(n²) always, no best case improvement. Makes exactly n(n−1)/2 comparisons regardless of input.
SpaceO(1), sorts in-place with at most n−1 swaps total
Key Insights
  • Core ideaEach pass finds the minimum of the unsorted portion and places it at the sorted boundary. Minimises total swaps, at most n−1 regardless of input.
  • When to useWhen write operations are expensive (e.g. flash memory with limited write cycles). Fewer swaps than bubble sort makes it practical in write-constrained environments.
  • Watch outUnlike insertion sort, it cannot detect an already-sorted array and stop early. It always completes all n(n−1)/2 comparisons.
